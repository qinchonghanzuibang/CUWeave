# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CUWeave a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private information and academic safety.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes occur.

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and other
conduct inappropriate in a professional or educational community are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
ban participants whose behavior is harmful. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact
method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy as far as
reasonably possible.

This policy is adapted in substance from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
