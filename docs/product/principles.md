# Product principles

- **Unofficial:** CUWeave is student-led and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CUHK.
- **Advisory:** CUSIS remains authoritative. Users must verify final enrollment details there.
- **No invention:** Academic facts and programme rules must never be guessed or fabricated.
- **Provenance:** Future academic records must retain their source and historical context.
- **Explicit uncertainty:** Ambiguous requirement or planning outcomes must be represented as
  uncertain, not forced into a confident answer.
- **Privacy minimization:** Collect only what a feature demonstrably needs.
- **Credential boundary:** Never collect CUHK passwords, OnePass credentials, student IDs, or
  transcripts.
- **Contributor clarity:** Prefer simple architecture and documented behavior that student
  contributors can run and understand.
